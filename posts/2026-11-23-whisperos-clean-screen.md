One of the best parts about using **WhisperOS** on my Heltec V3 is honestly just how clean the screen is. Most firmware for the little LoRa boards either tries to cram way too much onto the tiny thing or gives you almost nothing so you have to pull out your phone for everything.

## Information on the screen

On a screen this small, too much info ruins it really fast. If there are tiny icons everywhere or ten different stats stacked on top of each other, I can't actually read any of it when you just glance down at your screen.

WhisperOS makes it **VERY** simple. It shows you the stuff you actually care about messages, signal/node info, and status without making the screen feel blah. You can look at it for one second and know what's happening.

## Being usable without a phone

Because the layout isn't overloaded, navigating around on the board itself feels more natural instead of like a chore. You don't feel forced to open an app on your phone just to check if something came through or see what your node is doing.

It's a very small thing, but having a dedicated  device with a screen that just tells you what you need cleanly makes it way more fun to carry around and use.
