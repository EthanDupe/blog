Every post on this site is a plain `.md` file inside the `posts/` folder.

## Adding a new post

1. Open [Write](write.html) in the top bar.
2. Write your post and click **Copy .md text** or **Download .md file**.
3. Add the `.md` file to the `posts/` folder in [GitHub](https://github.com/EthanDupe/blog).

Your new post will show up here automatically—no build step needed.

## Supported Markdown

- Headings (`##` and `###`)
- **Bold**, *italic*, ~~strikethrough~~, and `inline code`
- Links, images, bullet lists, numbered lists, and blockquotes
